How this report works

What this report does

Validates the MTD Production export in two layers. The Summary sheet is the month-to-date view. Club tabs hold one row per day.

Summary logic

  • Active + Frozen + Pending Cancel + PIF Count = Grand Total, in count and in dollars.
  • Paid in full dues are $0.
  • Top membership counts, and the five paymode counts, each equal Grand Total. Paymode has no total row.
  • Billing membership counts equal Active Members.
  • Percents in each block equal 100%.
  • Gender counts equal Grand Total.
  • The twelve annual-fee months equal the annual Grand Total.
  • Daily Summary carries the same member Grand Total as Summary.
  • Club gross-invoice dollars equal the Summary Gross Invoices total.
  • In Club, Web, and Mobile type columns equal that channel’s Total, and the three channels equal the combined Total.
  • Classic Plus (CLASPL) is its own bucket. No duplicate club. A club with invoices still has joins. ACH% + CC% equals 100% when the club has members or invoices.

Date coverage

  • Read every club sheet.
  • For IN CLUB JOINS, VOLUNTARY CANCELS, and Transfers In, collect the Date rows.
  • Build the calendar from the earliest date in the file through the latest.
  • Highlight a club when a day is missing, or when the section has no date rows at all.
